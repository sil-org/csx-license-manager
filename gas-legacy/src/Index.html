Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

What is License Manager? +
License Manager is the central place to browse, subscribe to, and manage software licenses available at SIL. It tracks who is using which tools, which budget is being charged, and handles the full approval and provisioning workflow between users, budget managers, and the GTIS team.
How do I subscribe to a license? +
Open the Catalog, click a license card to see its details, then click Subscribe. You will confirm the email the license is for (defaults to your own) and choose how to pay — SuiteKey or Ministry Account (for non-GTIS licenses).

What happens next depends on the license:
  • If you are the SuiteKey owner, or it is GTIS-funded with no manual setup required, the subscription becomes Active immediately.
  • If approval is needed, the budget manager is emailed and your subscription shows as Pending Approval.
  • If the vendor side requires a seat to be provisioned first, it shows as Approved — Pending Activation until GTIS sets it up.
Can I subscribe on behalf of someone else? +
Yes. When the subscription form opens, the License for field is pre-filled with your own email — just change it to the recipient’s address. The subscription will be recorded under their account and they will be notified at each stage.
What do the different subscription statuses mean? +
Pending SK VerificationWaiting for a suggested SuiteKey manager to confirm they own that budget.
Pending ApprovalThe budget manager has been notified and needs to approve or decline.
Approved — Pending ActivationApproved; GTIS is setting up your account on the vendor side. You will receive an email when it is ready.
ActiveYour license is live and ready to use.
Declined / RevokedDeclined by the budget manager, or revoked by an admin.

You can withdraw any pending or not-yet-activated subscription at any time from My Subscriptions.

Billing

What is a SuiteKey? +
A SuiteKey is a budget cost center code. When you subscribe to a non-GTIS license, you select the SuiteKey that should be charged — the SuiteKey’s budget manager then approves or declines the request. Just enter the code; you do not need the ~XXXX suffix.
What if my SuiteKey is not listed, or has no manager? +
If your SuiteKey is not found, or has no manager assigned, you will be asked for the email of the person who manages that budget. They will receive a verification email to confirm or deny ownership. If they confirm, they are registered as the manager and your request moves into the normal approval queue. If they deny it, you will be notified to resubmit with the correct contact.
What is a Ministry Account? +
A Ministry Account is a personal 6-digit billing identifier for staff whose costs are covered by a ministry rather than a SuiteKey. If you have a Ministry Account on file it will be pre-filled automatically when you subscribe — no approval is required. Licenses purchased with a Ministry Account are for use by SIL staff only. Contact the License Manager team if your Ministry ID is missing or needs to be corrected.
What does “GTIS-funded” mean? +
GTIS centrally covers the cost of certain enterprise licenses. If a license shows the GTIS badge, no SuiteKey or Ministry Account is required — just subscribe and the cost is handled centrally. Some GTIS licenses activate immediately; others require a seat to be provisioned first.
Are subscriptions refundable? +
Subscriptions are non-refundable once activated. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not issue a credit for the current billing period. If you are unsure whether you need a license, you can withdraw a pending or not-yet-activated subscription before it goes active.

Managing Subscriptions

How do I cancel a subscription? +
Go to My Subscriptions and click Cancel (active) or Withdraw (pending or approved-not-yet-activated). If the license uses a Google Group, you will be removed automatically. Cancellations are non-refundable once the subscription is active.
How does renewal work? +
All annual licenses get reminders starting two months before their renewal date, with weekly follow-ups until you respond. Each reminder includes direct Yes, Renew and No, Don’t Renew links — no need to open the app. You can also respond anytime from My Subscriptions.

For Budget Managers & People Managers

I own a SuiteKey — how do I manage approval requests? +
The SuiteKey Charges tab appears automatically if you are registered as a SuiteKey owner. A badge appears when you have pending requests. From there you can approve or decline requests, view all active subscriptions charged to your SuiteKey(s), and review the audit log. You will also receive a reminder email every Monday morning for any requests that are still unanswered.
I am a people manager — where can I see my team’s subscriptions? +
If you have direct reports in the staff directory, My Team appears automatically. It lists all subscriptions for your direct and indirect reports so you can see at a glance who is using which tools.

Support

How do I get help or send feedback? +
Use the Send Feedback button at the bottom of the page to reach the License Manager team directly. For questions about a specific subscription or charge, include the subscription ID shown in My Subscriptions — it makes it much faster to look up.
